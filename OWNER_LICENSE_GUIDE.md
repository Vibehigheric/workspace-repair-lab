## Important limits

GitHub permits platform viewing and forking of public repositories. A custom license cannot take back permissions you grant through GitHub's terms. Public code is not a trade secret merely because it carries a restrictive license. Publish the small demo, and keep valuable production tools, client configurations and secrets private.

This kit was generated with AI assistance. US copyright requires sufficient human authorship; a notice does not create exclusive rights in purely AI-generated content. Record actual human contributions, revisions and provenance. Do not claim registration, exclusively human authorship or ownership of third-party work without evidence. Counsel should review the actual rights available and whether contractual protections are appropriate.

A README alone is not proof a customer agreed to liability or payment terms. For paid work, use a signed agreement or an appropriate affirmative acceptance process identifying the exact dated terms. Preserve the agreed version and acceptance record. There is no clickwrap or payment acceptance system implemented in this kit.

The evaluation restriction means you must issue a separate commercial grant when customers buy deployment rights. Do not sell production use while leaving them with evaluation-only permission.

## Before the first paid job

1. Confirm legal business name and applicable business requirements; an email address does not establish an entity or separate personal liability.
2. Agree the computer/project, hours cap, price, exclusions, payment/refund terms and acceptance tests.
3. Obtain express authority for access, changes, backups, restarts and downtime; customer retains control of credentials and MFA.
4. Define pre-existing tools versus customer-owned material and specify deliverable rights in writing.
5. Define secure log transfer, retention/deletion, confidentiality and permission before any portfolio use.
6. Have counsel review the license and service agreement; consider appropriate professional/cyber insurance with a broker.
7. Review the ZIP before publication. The older unlicensed ZIP is superseded for future distribution but remains preserved locally.
